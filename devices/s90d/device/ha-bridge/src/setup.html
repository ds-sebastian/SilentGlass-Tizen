Advanced settings

Living Room TV

Shortcuts

The inputs on the bar, in this order. Tap an icon to change it. A shortcut set to Hidden isn't on the bar; under each name the TV shows the input's own name.

Home screen

The wallpaper follows sunset at this location (city level is plenty; without one, the TV's time zone is used). Over a picture the menu closes by itself after this long without a key press; over the wallpaper it stays, and the wallpaper dims (OLED care).

Notifications

Add the TV in Home Assistant with the "Notifications for Android TV" integration. "As sent" keeps what each automation asks for. Transparency covers the whole banner, picture included. Input alerts are the small note when a device is plugged in or unplugged. Pictures are camera snapshots and other images. In quiet hours notifications are dropped, not saved for later. Notifications are accepted from the MQTT server's address and the ones listed here, and never wake the TV. The test uses the settings on this page, before you save.

Privacy and updates

Unverified

On blocks only the outside Internet. Everything on your home network keeps working: Home Assistant, notifications, Thread and this page. It removes the TV's route out (its own network settings, no gateway), so Samsung's cloud, apps, ads and updates can't connect. It holds through restarts. If it ever gets stuck: Settings › General › Network › Network Status › IP Settings › Obtain automatically. A privacy and update switch, not a speed boost.

Thread border router

Checking…

The TV can be a Thread border router for your smart home. To join your network it needs the network's dataset: let it read Home Assistant's preferred network once (the token is used for that and then forgotten), or paste the dataset (Home Assistant: Settings › Thread › your network › ⓘ).

Home Assistant

Checking…

Your MQTT broker (Home Assistant's Mosquitto add-on, or your own). The TV appears in Home Assistant by itself. The password stays on the TV; leave it empty to keep the current one.

Check volume and names every

Power, input and the app on screen are sent the moment they change. Volume, mute and input names are checked this often; longer saves the TV a little work. Temperature and picture settings: every minute.

This TV

The TV's own name, the same as Settings › General › System Manager › Device Name: what phones, SmartThings, Bluetooth and Home Assistant show.
